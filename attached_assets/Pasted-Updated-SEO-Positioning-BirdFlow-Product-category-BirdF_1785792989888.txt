Updated SEO Positioning — BirdFlow
Product category

BirdFlow is a digital website, booking and practice-management platform for independent practitioners and clinics working with mental health, therapy and personal wellbeing.

The platform can be used by:

Psychologists
Psychiatrists
Psychotherapists
Therapists
Couples therapists
Family therapists
Trauma therapists
Stress therapists
Coaches working with mental wellbeing
Counsellors
Mental-health clinics
Multidisciplinary treatment clinics
Alternative practitioners
Holistic therapists
Healers

BirdFlow should not be presented as a generic website builder for every type of business.

It should be presented as a specialized digital platform for practitioners whose work depends on trust, professional presentation, clear information, booking and client communication.

Important positioning distinction

BirdFlow may serve both regulated healthcare professionals and non-regulated practitioners.

The platform must not imply that all practitioners have the same qualifications, legal status or healthcare authorization.

Examples:

Psychiatrists are medical doctors.
Psychologists may have protected professional titles and authorization.
Psychotherapists, therapists, coaches and healers may operate under different educational, professional and regulatory conditions.

BirdFlow must store and display each practitioner’s actual title, credentials and memberships without inventing or standardizing them.

Do not label all customers as healthcare professionals.

Do not describe healers or alternative practitioners as medical providers unless that accurately reflects their legal and professional status.
