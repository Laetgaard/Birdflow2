I would also cut #198 down

Replit's statement:

“GDPR/privacy policy generator — without this they can't legally take on clients”

is misleading.

A privacy notice is important. Datatilsynet says organizations must tell people how their information is processed and that this can be provided through a privacy policy when collecting information.

But generating a privacy policy does not make the practitioner GDPR compliant.

Particularly when Birdflow processes mental-health information: Datatilsynet classifies physical and mental health data as sensitive personal data, and such processing needs an applicable exception for sensitive information in addition to the general lawful-processing framework.

So don't add:

✅ GDPR compliant

because Birdflow generated three paragraphs.
