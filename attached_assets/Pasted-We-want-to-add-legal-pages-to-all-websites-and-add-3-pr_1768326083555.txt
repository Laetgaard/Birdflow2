We want to add legal pages to all websites and add 3 professional starter templates with real content and structure.

PART 1 — LEGAL PAGES FOR ALL WEBSITES

Goal:
Every website must automatically include:
- /terms (Terms of Service)
- /privacy (Privacy Policy)

Implementation:
1) Add 2 system pages to every website:
   - Terms
   - Privacy

2) Pages use a simple editable template with placeholders:
   - {{website_name}}
   - {{company_name}}
   - {{email}}
   - {{address}}

3) Add Manage → Legal section:
   - Fields:
     - Website name
     - Company name
     - Email
     - Address
   - These values auto-fill the legal pages.

4) Provide default generic legal text.
5) Pages must:
   - Be part of builder_state
   - Be included in footer or navigation
   - Be published with the site
   - Be fully editable in the builder
